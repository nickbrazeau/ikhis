In brief, mRNA was purified from 400 ng of total RNA using oligo-dT beads, and cDNA was synthesized according to NuGEN’s protocol. The AnyDeplete kit (NuGEN) was used to remove ribosomal RNA (rRNA) and globin transcripts before library amplification. The purified libraries were quantified with the Qubit dsDNA HS kit (Thermo Fisher Scientific), and their size and distribution were analyzed using a Bioanalyzer with the High Sensitivity DNA kit (Agilent). After library preparation, samples were pooled, and library integrity was verified by sequencing on an Illumina MiSeq. Deep sequencing was then conducted on a NovaSeq 6000 (Illumina, Inc., San Diego, CA), with an average depth of 30 million 100 bp paired-end reads per sample.
