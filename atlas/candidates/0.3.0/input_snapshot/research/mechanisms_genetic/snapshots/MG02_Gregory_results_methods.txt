L354@P4: The effect of rs6897932 on differential splicing of IL7R
L355@P4: The alternative splicing of IL7R has potent consequences for the
L356@P4: function of the receptor, as transcripts that include exon 6 encode a
L357@P4: membrane-bound IL7Ra, whereas transcripts that skip exon 6 encode
L358@P4: a soluble form of the protein14. To test whether the alternate ‘C’
L359@P4: and ‘T’ alleles of rs6897932 could affect the inclusion of exon 6,
L360@P4: we cloned the two allelic versions of exon 6 and the surrounding
L361@P4: intronic sequences into the alternative splicing minigene construct
L362@P4: pI-11 (ref. 15). Analysis of transcripts from the two minigenes showed
L363@P4: that the multiple sclerosis–associated ‘C’ allele resulted in an approximately twofold increase in the skipping of exon 6 compared with
L364@P4: transcripts containing the ‘T’ allele (Fig. 2). The data are consistent
L365@P4: with at least two possible mechanisms: the multiple sclerosis–
L366@P4: associated ‘C’ allele weakens an exonic splicing enhancer (ESE),
L367@P4: or it augments an exonic splicing silencer (ESS). The latter is more
L368@P4: likely, as transversions at rs6897932 to either a G or an A nucleotide
L369@P4: substantially decreased exon skipping (for example, production of
L370@P4: soluble IL7Ra), and substitutions adjacent to the SNP essentially
L371@P4: abolished skipping (Fig. 2). Thus, our results are consistent with
L372@P4: the presence of a weak ESS that mediates a low level of exon 6
L373@P4: skipping, resulting in about 10% soluble IL7Ra. Our data suggest a
L374@P4: functional role for the alternative rs6897932 alleles on the splicing of
L375@P4: exon 6 in IL7R.
L376@P4: Allele-specific expression of IL7R
L377@P4: We analyzed peripheral blood mononuclear cells from 94 healthy
L378@P4: controls by quantitative real-time PCR to detect differential expression
L379@P4: of IL7R using one amplicon that spanned exons 6–7 and two control
L380@P4: amplicons spanning exons 4–5 and exons 7–8, respectively. Using
L381@P4: analysis of variance with Bonferroni correction, we observed significantly lower expression of the exon 6–7 amplicon for carriers of the
L382@P4: multiple sclerosis–associated ‘C’ allele (P ¼ 0.015); the two control
L383@P4: amplicons (exons 4–5 and 7–8) did not show significantly different
L384@P4: expression by genotype. Consistent with our in vitro experiments, this
L385@P4: suggests that carriers of the ‘C’ allele at rs6897932 produce less
L386@P4: membrane-bound IL7Ra protein compared with carriers of the
L387@P4: ‘T’ allele, leading to a further increase of the soluble form of IL7Ra.
L388@P4: This hypothesis is supported by our previous report that interleukin-7
L389@P4: (IL7) function and IL7Ra expression in T cells is reduced in individuals with multiple sclerosis16.
