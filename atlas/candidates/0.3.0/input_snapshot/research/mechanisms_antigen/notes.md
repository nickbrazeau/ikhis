# Antigen processing and presentation: extraction candidate pack

Role: evidence extraction only. Independent review and human review remain pending the user's trigger. No numerical priors, production changes or release freeze were performed. Scope covers broad immune biology beyond any cytokine inventory; no cytokine priority set was applied.

Seven primary articles support 31 narrow assertions. Nineteen connection proposals distinguish three narrow perturbation/rescue dependencies, nonserial response/contrast profiles, and five blocked connections. All assertions are `extracted_pending_review`; this is a bounded ranked-search pilot, not an exhaustive review.

The clearest human-effector rescue is B2M replacement in the patient-derived melanoma line 1074mel: intracellular B2M and surface class I return, and compatible human antimelanoma CD8 effectors lyse the target. The effectors were explicitly not autologous. The target is melanoma culture, not a primary immune APC. The HLA-mismatched 1259mel control remains near background. The separate five-line vaccinia assay uses mouse H-2Kd and mouse CD8 recipients; it is not five human-recipient replications. Surface-HLA increase and killing remain a shared-intervention profile; selective surface-HLA mediation and particular peptide-HLA abundance were not measured.

Human TAP complementation is gene-specific: TAP1 restores class I on TND-3 EBV-B cells where TAP2 does not. TAP2 complementation restores HA-L surface class I, but uncomplemented HA-L can already be lysed by the patient's expanded EBV-reactive CD8 cells. Reduced class-I abundance therefore cannot stand in for total loss of antigen recognition. The TAP2 splice mutation and abnormal cDNA are observed directly, while a mutant-protein transition remains unmeasured. BMRF1/HLA-B7 specificity was assigned using nonhuman COS target cells and a human CTL/TNF-bioactivity assay. The article's Methods and Fig5b timing descriptions conflict; no harmonized timing is invented.

Primary-human-monocyte-derived DCs provide a useful layer contrast. LPS reduces CIITA RNA, CIITA protein and later HLA-DRA RNA, while surface HLA-DR increases. The source documents different CIITA isoform persistence and does not support a quantitative RNA-to-protein equivalence. Donor identity across RNA, immunoblot and flow experiments is unavailable. TSA attenuates CIITA RNA silencing but is a broad inhibitor. No antigen-specific human CD4 endpoint is extracted from this study.

ERAP1 knockout/reconstitution and precursor-length tests support an ERAP1-dependent recognition mechanism in engineered cell systems. WM793 and WM278 are melanoma lines. The recipient is a mouse T-hybridoma bearing a selected human psoriatic TCR. Hap2 trims the 11mer substrate faster than Hap10, but HPLC does not distinguish 9mer from 8mer sufficiently, and no biochemical-digest transfer into the cellular assay is demonstrated. DT-9 surface staining has stated cross-reactivity. The 12mer Hap2-versus-Hap10 difference was not significant. These limits block a fully measured human-primary-cell peptide-to-function chain.

Kuilman 2025 supplies a recent, explicitly human-cell-host specificity contrast. B2M knockout reduces activation of MEL9 TCR1 in engineered Jurkat recipients, whereas TCR22 remains active and is inhibited by HLA-II antibody. The B cells are immortalized; Jurkat is a reporter line, and its engineered TCR constant domains are mouse-derived. No primary effector cytotoxicity or post-knockout surface-HLA quantification is claimed.

He 2025 supplies primary human B-cell depletion/add-back and autologous PBMC proliferation evidence. Depletion/add-back measures bulk thymidine incorporation in seven patients and seven controls. The CD8 CFSE result uses 17 patients and 9 controls; exact donor overlap and CD8-resolved response under the B-cell manipulation are unavailable. Pan-HLA-I blockade acts on multiple PBMC populations and does not isolate B-cell HLA-I. Tonsillar B-cell stimulation of the selected mouse reporter cannot be transported to the endogenous human blood CD8 repertoire. The four B-cell-line immunopeptidomes did not detect the ADAMTSL5 peptide, which limits transplanting the melanocyte autoantigen mechanism. Nondetection is not absence. W6/32 purified pan-HLA ligands; HLA-C assignments were computational. HG00131/HG00313 and 1%-FDR/FDR_0.1-filename inconsistencies remain explicit questions.

The same ST-EMO control recurs across TAP studies. The same selected psoriatic TCR lineage and some cell lines recur across the ERAP1/B-cell studies. Tonsils were previously described in a cited publication. Technical replicates, repeated appearances, related publications and cell lines are not counted as independent donor evidence. Exact selected-paper identifiers were not found in existing research packs during a local scan, but whole-project donor independence remains unresolved.
