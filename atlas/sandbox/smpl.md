Focus exclusively on constructing a source-backed cytokine–immune-cell interaction knowledge base that can serve as a probabilistic biological prior. The desired network schema is: source cell → cytokine → target cell (which can be source cell) → receptor complex → proximal signaling pathway → transcriptional response → immediate cellular process. The review should: prioritize canonical and recent 2024–2026 reviews, perturbation atlases and curated databases; distinguish human from animal evidence; capture tissue, disease, experimental and temporal context; separate direct perturbational evidence from inferred ligand–receptor communication; assign uncertainty and evidence quality to every edge; identify activating, inhibitory, chemotactic, survival, differentiation and cell-death effects; develop a reproducible search strategy, inclusion criteria and extraction schema; produce a model-ready interaction table with full provenance. Important anchor sources already identified include the 2024 Immune Dictionary, the 2025 Human Cytokine Dictionary preprint, LIANA+, CellPhoneDB, OmniPath, Reactome, SIGNOR, CytoSig and related resources.
