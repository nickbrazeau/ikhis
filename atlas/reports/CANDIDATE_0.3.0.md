# Steps 1–3: candidate ready for review

Prepared September 13, 2026. The fixed cytokine priority coverage set is removed from the active README, protocol and project scope. All cytokines and broader immune mechanisms are eligible. Historical assay labels remain provenance only.

The [review packet](../candidates/0.3.0/REVIEW_PACKET.md) contains 76 new assertions across 19 source records, 42 cohort/experimental-context records and 24 mechanism dossiers. Seventy-four assertions have inspected primary Methods and Results; two remain explicitly marked as needing full text. Three source records revisit papers already in the baseline and do not count as independent new publications. The 83 screened candidate appearances come from documented focused searches, not an exhaustive systematic retrieval.

|---|---|

| 2. Deepen evidence and close source gaps | Three source-linked extraction dossiers, screening decisions, original snapshots, donor/preparation distinctions, correction checks and contrary observations. The ETS2 primary-source access gap is resolved and four narrow macrophage assertions are added. |
| 3. Assemble defensible connections | A queryable candidate database and typed graphs with 47 connection proposals: 8 narrow mediation candidates, 14 shared-intervention profiles, 14 context contrasts and 11 blocked connections. All await scientific review. |

The initial all-cytokine discovery inventory contains 243 reviewed UniProt resource entries and 383 unreviewed entries from complete human cytokine-annotation queries. These are resource records, not 626 distinct active cytokines. The inventory creates no biological assertion or numerical prior and does not exclude cytokines, isoforms or complexes found through later literature curation.

The new material reaches beyond cytokine identity lookup. It includes DDX39B/IL7R splicing and rescue experiments; CD81 and enhancer perturbations in primary T cells; ETS2 regulation and measured macrophage function; AP-1, STAT5, IL-2 and FOXP3 activation/regulatory experiments; and B2M, TAP, CIITA, ERAP1 and antigen-specific recognition systems. Distinct human, engineered and nonhuman preparations remain explicit. RNA, protein, surface complexes and function are separate measurements.

Original validation passed: 65 tests, no credential metadata detected by the scanner at that time, intact candidate source dependencies, and an offline rebuild matching all 147 candidate artifacts from 129 saved inputs even after copied file timestamps were changed. See the [original reproduction record](CANDIDATE_REPRODUCTION_0.3.0.json). Every table and record inherited from Release 0.2.0 is unchanged, including its uncalibrated priors and review history. No new numerical priors or approved production pathways were created.

Security maintenance on 15 September 2026 removed a third-party Google Maps key embedded in a publisher's HTML and rebuilt the candidate with updated provenance hashes. The scientific records and frozen releases remain unchanged. See [alert #2 remediation](SECURITY_ALERT_2.md) and [the redaction audit](../provenance/security_redactions_2026-09-15.json); the earlier reproduction record describes the pre-redaction candidate.

The next step is the review the user will trigger separately. The packet identifies specific questions about source fidelity, narrow mediation claims, donor overlap, primary versus engineered systems, whole-blood/PBMC preparation, bioassay specificity and recent-paper metadata inconsistencies. Two RBPJ assertions still lack complete primary experimental details. Blocked serial transitions remain evidence gaps; they cannot be repaired by treating shared responses as causal chains.

After that review, broader systematic corpus completion, human adjudication, empirical calibration and model-facing validation remain necessary for the full end product. This candidate completes the requested preparation milestone, not the entire immune-system evidence program.
