# Project implementation and scientific completion status

All cytokines and broader immune genetic, transcriptional, proteomic, signaling and cellular mechanisms are in scope. The fixed cytokine priority coverage set is retired. Resource inventory size and historical assay labels are not completion denominators.

Steps 1–3 are prepared in the [0.3.0 candidate review packet](../candidates/0.3.0/REVIEW_PACKET.md): 76 pending-review assertions, 19 source records, 42 cohort/context records, 83 candidate appearances and 24 mechanism dossiers. The inventory has 626 resource entries, with reviewed and unreviewed resource annotations distinguished; this is not a count of distinct active cytokines.

The connections comprise 8 mechanism proposals, 14 shared-intervention profiles, 14 context contrasts and 11 blocked proposals. These are extraction-stage distinctions, not scientific approvals. New assertions are stored separately from the accepted baseline. No new priors or approved production paths were created.

The next action is the separately triggered review: inspect sources, narrow mechanisms, sample overlap, molecular states, contrary results and proposed corrections. Broader systematic corpus completion, human adjudication, empirical calibration and model validation remain later work. No independent review has been started for this candidate.
