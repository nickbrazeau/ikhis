Supplementary Fig. 3 | De novo TCR identification from TIL and recovery of TCRs from the TCR library expression cassette. a, Bar graph of the number of TCR chain clonotypes identified from 3 fresh-frozen non-viable melanoma samples, with two technical replicates (#3-1 and #3-2). b, Correlation plots for TCRα chains between the replicates of tumor sample 3 from a. Shared clonotype frequencies in both replicates and the Pearson correlation coefficient are represented. c, As in b but for TCRβ chains. d, TCR expression cassettes (~1500 bp) were retrieved from genomic DNA by a multi-round PCR strategy. TapeStation analysis in lane ‘1’ represents the second round PCR product, whereas ‘M’ indicates the marker lane. e, A library preparation with one sample barcode was performed on the PCR product from d and was sequenced using ONT. After analysis using the guppy software package, sample misassignment rates were calculated as a function of the barcode score threshold applied during analysis. f, Reads from e were aligned to a combined reference containing sequences from the matching TCR and from 99 non-matching TCRs. Clonotype misassignment rates are represented without (-) or with the filtering (Filter) strategy described in Methods. g, The indicated TCR plasmids were mixed at varying frequencies in 9 plasmid mixes, and TCR chain frequencies were quantified as in d-f and are represented for each of the 9 samples. h, The median read coverage per TCR, dropout TCRs that were not detected, and the percentage of TCR combinations that were represented within a 4-fold range around the median are summarized for the data from Fig. 3c. i, The TCR library from Fig. 3d was retrovirally transduced into reporter T cells. The percentage of TCR-transduced reporter T cells before and after puromycin selection is represented as measured by murine TCRβ constant domain expression using FACS. j, Reporter T cells expressing antigen-specific TCRs at indicated frequencies were cocultured with EBV-immortalized B cells expressing the cognate antigens at indicated frequencies. TCR expression cassettes were retrieved and quantified from non-activated and activated T cell samples as described in d-f, and the relative enrichment is represented. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

Supplementary Fig. 4 | Identification and characterization of neoantigen-specific TCRs from a melanoma sample. a, B cells expressing one of 48 TMGs were mixed into the 14 indicated pools (R1-R7 and C1-C7) to allow combinatorial TMG encoding. + indicates positive control B cells expressing a mutated CDK4 epitope. b, The number of TCR chain clonotypes in a fresh-frozen non-viable melanoma specimen. c, Read support of the clonotypes in b relative to their rank order. d, TCR representation in combinatorial TCR sub-libraries assembled from the 199 most prevalent TCRα and β chains in b and from the TCR chains of a positive control TCR CDK4-17. e, Quality control summary statistics of sub-libraries in d as described in Methods. f, TCR libraries from d were retrovirally transduced into reporter T cells. The percentage of TCR-transduced reporter T cells before and after blasticidin selection is represented as measured by murine TCRβ constant domain expression using FACS. g, Upon coculture of cells from f with B cell pools from a and subsequent cell sorting as in Fig. 4a, TCR expression cassettes were amplified from gDNA. TapeStation analysis of the ~1500 bp second round PCR products are represented. ‘M’ indicates a marker lane, and pairwise non-activated and activated samples from C1-C7 and R1-R7 pools are loaded from left to right, respectively. h, Reporter T cells expressing TCRs recognizing non-frameshift mutations from Fig. 4c were cocultured with B cells or these cells electroporated with 100 µM mutant or wild-type 25-mer peptides or these cells loaded with 10 µM mutant or wild-type 9-mer peptides (TCRs 17-21). T cell activation was assessed by CD69 expression on reporter T cells by FACS. Non-transduced B cells were included as control. T cell activation was assessed by CD69 expression by FACS in two biological replicates. Error bars represent the standard deviation. Supplementary Fig. 5 | Identification of candidate TCRs from a set of microsatellite-stable colorectal cancers. a, Tumor RNA derived from fresh-frozen tumor tissue of three microsatellite stable (MSS) colorectal carcinoma (CRC) patients was subjected to TCR clonotype identification. The number of identified TCR chain clonotypes is represented. b, The 100 most prevalent TCR chains from a were used for combinatorial TCR library assembly. The table represents quality control summary statistics of the libraries (see Methods). c, The TCR libraries from b were retrovirally transduced into Jurkat reporter T cells, and the percentage of TCR-transduced reporter T cells before and after blasticidin selection was measured by the murine TCRβ constant domain expression using FACS. d, Pt3 and pt15 TCR libraries were screened in triplicate against a pool of TMG expressing B cell lines, in one replicate against non-transduced B cells, and in one replicate against each individual TMG expressing B cell line. A FACS-sort based approach was used for isolation of activated and non-activated reporter T cells. The Pt4 TCR library was screened using a combinatorial TMG pooling approach (‘+’ indicates a minigene expressing a mutated CDK4 epitope) and a MACS based approach was applied for cell sorting. A positive control TCR CDK4-17 was mixed in at 1:10,000 frequency. e, Genomic DNA was isolated from pt3 sorted cell populations from screens in d. A TapeStation analysis is shown from the second round PCR of the TCR expression cassette amplification. ‘M’ indicates marker lane, while ‘-’ denotes water controls. The numbers represent pairs of non-activated and activated T cell samples. f, As in e, but for pt4. g, As in e, but for pt15. h, Heatmap of Pearson correlation coefficients of the TCR read counts between all samples from e after library preparation, sequencing and read identification. i, As in h, but for samples from f. j, As in h, but for samples from g. k, Plots showing the TCR reactivity following coculture with autologous immortalized B cells in the absence and presence of exogenous antigen expression for pt3, pt4 and pt15 screens. Candidate TCRs are represented in green. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.

Supplementary Fig. 6 | Validation and characterization of neoantigen-specific TCRs from a set of microsatellite-stable colorectal cancers. a, As in Supplementary Fig. 5k (pt3), but for a coculture with B cells expressing a single TMG (TMG1 or 2) in each screen. b, As in a, but for individual TMGs for pt15. c, (Left) reporter T cells expressing the candidate TCR from pt3 were cocultured with autologous immortalized B cells electroporated with 10 μM of the 25-mer peptides encoded in TMG1. Supplementary Fig. 7 | Identification of TIL-derived TCRs from a microsatellite-stable colorectal cancer. a, Identification of TCRα and β chain clonotypes in fresh-frozen non-viable tumor tissue from a microsatellite-stable colorectal cancer patient. Read support of the clonotypes relative to their rank order is represented on a double logarithmic scale. b, The rank order based on read support for all validated and deconvoluted TCRs identified for all patients that are screened (see Table 1). In addition, an aggregate of all samples (‘All’) was made. c, Reporter T cells expressing the CDK4_17 TCR were cocultured with autologous immortalized B cells electroporated with 10 μM 25-mer peptide, transduced with a TMG encoding the same peptide sequence or the full-length (FL) gene. B cells without the antigen (B cells) served as a control. T cell activation was assessed by CD69 expression on reporter T cells by FACS. Bars represent the average value of two biological replicates and error bars represent the standard deviation. d, As in c, but for the MEL15 GSS TCR. e, TCR clustering analysis using GIANA on the patient samples from Fig. 4 (MEL9) and Fig. 5 (CRC17). Orange filled squares (nodes) represent TCRs, while blue, red and green lines (edges) represent TCRs that have been experimentally validated to share a neoantigen target (‘measured’), have been predicted by GIANA to share a neoantigen target (‘predicted’) or have been measured and predicted, respectively. f, TCR clustering analysis using the minimal Levenshtein edit distance method on the patient samples from Fig. 4 (MEL9) and Fig. 5 (CRC17). Orange filled squares (nodes) represent TCRs, while blue, red and green lines (edges) represent TCRs that have been experimentally validated to share a neoantigen target (‘measured’), have been predicted by GIANA to share a neoantigen target (‘predicted’) or have been measured and predicted, respectively. g, The number of predicted and measured edges (‘correctly predicted edges’), the number of these that share an identical alpha or beta chain, and the percentage of correctly predicted edges that share identical chains are represented per antigen and summed over all antigens (‘All’) from the data in e and f. Source data are provided as a Source Data file.
